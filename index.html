    How to play

    • Drag a piece onto the board — or tap it, then tap where you want it.
    • Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear several at once for far more points.
    • Clearing on consecutive moves builds a combo.
    • You get 3 assists a game. Spend them on hints, on undo, or a mix.
    • Undo goes back one move — even from Game Over.
